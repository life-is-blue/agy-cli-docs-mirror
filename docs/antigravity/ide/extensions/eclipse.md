# Eclipse

The **Google Antigravity** extension, available on the [Eclipse Marketplace](https://marketplace.eclipse.org/content/google-antigravity), brings the Antigravity agent directly into Eclipse. Delegate complex coding tasks, run multi-step agentic workflows, and review proposed file changes without leaving your Eclipse workspace.

* * *

## Prerequisites

Ensure that you meet the following prerequisites before installing the extension:

*   **Eclipse**: [Eclipse](https://www.eclipse.org/downloads/) 2026-06 or later on macOS, Linux, or Windows.
*   **Antigravity entitlement**: a Google Account with any Antigravity plan (including the free tier) or an enterprise account enabled for **[Gemini Enterprise](/docs/enterprise)**.

* * *

## Installation and setup

Complete the following steps to install the Google Antigravity extension in Eclipse:

1.  Open Eclipse.
2.  Click **Help** in the top menu bar, then select **Eclipse Marketplace…** from the drop-down menu.
3.  Search for **Google Antigravity** (published by **Antigravity Eclipse Publisher**).
4.  Click **Install** and finish the installation wizard.
5.  Restart Eclipse when prompted. On first launch after installation, the **Antigravity** view opens automatically on the right side of the editor area. Confirm that you trust the project folders to continue.

To open or hide the **Antigravity** view at any time, select **Antigravity** > **Toggle Chat** in the top menu bar (or press Cmd + Option + E on macOS or Ctrl + Alt + K on Windows and Linux).

* * *

## Authentication and sign-in

On first launch, follow the sign-in prompt in the Antigravity panel to authenticate in your browser:

*   **Individual accounts**: sign in with your personal Google Account.
*   **Enterprise accounts**: click **Sign in with Gemini Enterprise**, authenticate with your corporate identity (including BYOID and Workforce Identity Federation), then select your organization’s **Google Cloud Project** and **Region** when prompted.

To switch accounts, change authentication methods, or configure AI model and MCP server preferences later, select **Antigravity** > **Settings…** in the top menu bar.
